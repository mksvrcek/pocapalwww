More in PocaPal

Every little thing,
thought through.

The small stuff that takes the admin out of collecting, so you can get back to the fun part.

  • Scan to add. Hold a card up to the camera and PocaPal works out the group, the album and which version you're holding, then files it. No typing, no picking from a list.

  • Add a whole haul at once. Point it at your camera roll and it pulls the individual cards out of your unboxing photos, so a new pull-set takes seconds rather than an evening.

  • See how close you are. Every album shows its completion at a glance, with the missing versions called out, so you know what you're actually hunting before you buy.

  • Dupes, kept separate. Flag a spare and it drops out of your main count but stays in your trade list, ready to send when someone's after it.

  • Search that knows what you mean. Filter by member, era, album or version, or ask for everything you're still missing from one release.

  • Share your binder. Send a link to your collection or your wishlist. Whoever opens it doesn't need the app, or an account, to look.

  • Works with no signal. Your collection lives on your phone, so it's there in the queue, at the venue, or halfway through a trade in a car park.
