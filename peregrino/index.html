Every trip gets
a luggage tag.

The countries it took in, stamped on. The dates, and a photo from the way. Open one and the route is on the globe, with the tickets, places and photos that went with it.

Tickets

gets a ticket.

Flights, trains, buses, ferries and walks print the moment you add them, and so do concerts and conferences. Pencilled in while you plan, counting down once booked, torn at the stub once you've been.
