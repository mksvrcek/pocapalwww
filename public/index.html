More in PocaPal

Every little thing.
Thought through.

The small stuff that takes the admin out of collecting, so you can get back to the fun part.

  • Scan to add. Hold a card up to the camera and PocaPal works out the group, the album and the version, then files it. No typing, no scrolling through lists.

  • Add a whole haul at once. Point it at your camera roll and it lifts each card out of your unboxing photos. A new pull takes seconds, not an evening.

  • See how close you are. Every album shows how complete it is, with the missing versions called out, so you know exactly what you're hunting before you buy.

  • Dupes, kept apart. Mark a spare and it leaves your main count but stays on your trade list, ready for whoever asks.

  • Search that knows what you mean. Filter by member, era, album or version, or pull up everything you're still missing from one release.

  • Share your binder. Send a link to your collection or your wishlist. Whoever opens it doesn't need the app, or an account, to have a look.

  • Works with no signal. Your collection lives on your phone, so it's there in the queue, at the venue, or halfway through a trade in a car park.
