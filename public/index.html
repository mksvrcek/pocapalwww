More in PocaPal

Every little thing.
Thought through.

The small stuff that takes the admin out of collecting, so you can get back to the fun part.

  • Binders you can page through. Arrange your cards into photobooks, pocket by pocket, and turn the pages just like the binder on your shelf.

  • Fanclub Scan a whole stack. Lay out a new haul and scan it once. Every card is cropped and paired front to back, ready for you to fill in.

  • Fanclub See what it’s worth. Watch your collection grow, and see what you’ve spent and what it’s worth now, by kind and by year.

  • Every artist, on file. Profiles for your groups and idols, with their members, their history and their top songs. Rank your favourites, and number one gets the spotlight.

  • Find any card in seconds. Search, then filter by group, idol or status, or show just your favourites or your duplicates.

  • Know what you’re hunting. Mark anything Owned, Preordered or Wishlist, so you know exactly what’s still missing before you get to the pop-up.

  • Show it off. Turn a card or a night out into a story for Instagram, or send your list as text.
